This is a **systems / HCI / Web3-infrastructure** paper, not a neural-architecture
paper. Target venues and audiences accordingly.

## How to reach the DeSoc authors

Tagging a bare link gets ignored. Lead with the demonstration: *"we took your
economic theory (DeSoc) and turned it into working code (AgentVCS) to solve AI's most
urgent problem (agent-swarm modal collapse)."* That is hard to ignore.

- **Vitalik Buterin** — far more responsive on **Farcaster (Warpcast)** than on X.
  Cast: *"In 2022, @vbuterin, Puja Ohlhaver & Glen Weyl proposed DeSoc for humans. We
  applied it to autonomous AI agents — cryptographic 'Souls' + SBTs — and used
  correlation discounting to stop agent swarms collapsing into monoculture. Code +
  litepaper: [link]."*
- **Puja Ohlhaver & Glen Weyl** — reach via the **RadicalxChange / Plurality** Discord
  and by replying to their threads on Plurality/AI on X. Message: *"@pujaohlhaver
  @glenweyl Your DeSoc paper inspired this. Correlation discounting is the exact
  mechanism that prevents AI agent swarms from collapsing into monoculture. Code +
  litepaper: [link] — would love your thoughts on Plural Intelligence for machines."*
- **SkillOpt team (Microsoft)** — *"We connected SkillOpt-Sleep to immutable, signed
  episodic memory. Now agent self-evolution has identity and provenance."*
